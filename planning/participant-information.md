# Information for registered participants

Text for the organisers to send to the participants who chose the workshop "From raw data to a website for humans" at the Data Curation Conference 2027 (Thursday 14 January 2027, 10:45 to 12:00). Drafted on 9 November 2026, revised on 10 November 2026 and reworked on 18 November 2026 (two steps, no dataset before the conference), to be sent as soon as registration opens. The steps live on the public pre-work website of the rawtoweb offer (repository rawtoweb/website, cohort path dcc-2027); the survey and the check-in issue must be live before the text goes out.

---

Thank you for choosing the workshop "From raw data to a website for humans: a live curation workflow with every AI step disclosed".

Everything you need before the day is on the workshop website: https://rawtoweb.github.io/website/dcc-2027/

There are two steps, both by Friday 8 January 2027.

Step 1 is the pre-course survey. It records your GitHub username and walks you through creating an account if you have none. Step 2 is one comment on the check-in issue of the workshop repository, with one line on the kind of data you work with. The website explains what an issue is and why we use it. The comment tests your account, and it is your first contribution to the dataset we build together in the workshop.

The workshop does not teach GitHub. We use issues and comments, and with them you learn how to contribute to a project on GitHub. Nobody sends a dataset before the conference. If you could imagine publishing a dataset of your own this way, tell me after the workshop.

Bring a laptop with its charger. No software has to be installed.

Questions before the day: lschoebitz@ethz.ch.

Lars Schöbitz, Global Health Engineering, ETH Zurich
