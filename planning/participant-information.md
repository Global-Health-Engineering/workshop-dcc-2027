# Information for registered participants

Text for the organisers to send to the participants who chose the workshop "From raw data to a website for humans" at the Data Curation Conference 2027 (Thursday 14 January 2027, 10:45 to 12:00). Drafted on 9 November 2026, to be sent as soon as registration opens. The steps live on the public pre-work website of the rawtoweb offer (repository rawtoweb/website, cohort path dcc-2027); the survey and the check-in issue must be live before the text goes out.

---

Thank you for choosing the workshop "From raw data to a website for humans: a live curation workflow with every AI step disclosed".

Everything you need before the day is on the workshop website: https://rawtoweb.github.io/website/dcc-2027/

Two steps are needed from everyone, by Friday 11 December 2026, and take about ten minutes together: a short pre-course survey, which records your GitHub username and walks you through creating an account if you have none, and one comment on the check-in issue of the workshop repository. The website explains what an issue is and why we use it. During the workshop you propose changes to the documentation of a dataset as comments on such an issue, and the changes are merged in front of the room with your name on them. The check-in comment tests your account and shows you where to type on the day.

Two steps are optional. You can install R and the washr package to build the same data package on your own laptop while the live run goes on. And you can submit a dataset by Friday 11 December 2026, your own or a published one under an open licence, which I publish as a data package with a DOI before the conference. The conditions are on the website.

Bring the laptop you did the steps on, with its charger. If a step fails for you, come anyway and pair with a neighbour at your table.

Questions before the day: lars.schoebitz@ethz.ch.

Lars Schöbitz, Global Health Engineering, ETH Zurich
