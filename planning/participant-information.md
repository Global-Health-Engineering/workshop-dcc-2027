# Information for registered participants

Text for the organisers to send to the participants who chose the workshop "From raw data to a website for humans" at the Data Curation Conference 2027 (Thursday 14 January 2027, 10:45 to 12:00). Drafted on 9 November 2026, revised on 10 November 2026, to be sent as soon as registration opens. The steps live on the public pre-work website of the rawtoweb offer (repository rawtoweb/website, cohort path dcc-2027); the survey and the check-in issue must be live before the text goes out.

---

Thank you for choosing the workshop "From raw data to a website for humans: a live curation workflow with every AI step disclosed".

Everything you need before the day is on the workshop website: https://rawtoweb.github.io/website/dcc-2027/

There are three steps, all by Friday 11 December 2026.

Steps 1 and 2 are required and take about ten minutes together. Step 1 is a short pre-course survey, which records your GitHub username and walks you through creating an account if you have none. Step 2 is one comment on the check-in issue of the workshop repository. The website explains what an issue is and why we use it. The comment tests your account and shows you where to type.

Step 3 is your dataset. Send your own data, or a published dataset under an open licence that you pick. I build a data package from it, you review it, and it is published with a website and a DOI before the conference, with you as an author. You then know the workflow from data you know before you enter the room, and the workshop has more time for the open questions. If you have no data you can share, you say so in the survey. The conditions are on the website.

Bring the laptop you did the steps on, with its charger. No software has to be installed.

Questions before the day: lars.schoebitz@ethz.ch.

Lars Schöbitz, Global Health Engineering, ETH Zurich
